## Feed prototype — her feedback

**Overall:** *"Overall, I really think it's good… the quality is there… quite a good MVP."* Positive and specific, not just polite.

1. **It solves her current direction problem.** Today on creatives she has to *"think of the angle myself, find the prompt myself, and then finally shoot it into ShopOS."* On GEO: *"I can insert my competition, but I don't know what to do after that."* The feed answers "where should I even go, direction-wise."
2. **It replaces the email round-trip.** The weekly AI-visibility update currently arrives by email, and she has to click back into ShopOS. On the feed she'd see it straight away.
3. **Mobile would suit high-level decisions.** She asked if a mobile app is planned. She'd use both: mobile to *save* and decide on the go, desktop to work.
4. **Trust line for new users:** brand details are fine because the owner can edit them. But anything that touches **current ad spend or the storefront** needs to feel *"more secure"*, and a new user will ask *"how confident are you that this would help the business?"*
5. **What would bring her back daily:** performance-marketing creatives first, GEO second. Storefront suggestions matter less to her because her catalog rarely changes.
6. **Her creative workflow (a concrete design spec):**
   - She likes/saves new creatives as they arrive. She does **not** push them straight to Meta.
   - Once a week she reviews her current ads, turns off the ones that aren't working, and *then* pushes new creatives.
   - What she asked for: *"that allowance to save it, and then at the end of every week, have the ability to directly push it all into the campaigns."* In short: **save → weekly bulk push.**
7. **Pro-feed signals:** retention/repeat-purchase signals don't fit her (one-time custom orders). They'd help a subscription brand like the protein-powder one. What matters to her: **sessions, product page → add-to-cart conversion, and which channels drive traffic into Shopify.** *"All this information is already on Shopify; the job for ShopOS is really to put it into actual decision-making points."*
8. She asked whether the team has shown the new design to other store owners.
